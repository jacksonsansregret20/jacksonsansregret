# International Market Entry Simulator

Research starter repository · 2 October 2026 · draft v0.1

**Working case:** Can-Am Defender utility side-by-side expansion through authorized distribution. Australia and Brazil are the initial evidence audit; Germany and Chile remain provisional comparison candidates.

This is a university decision-support project. It does not represent BRP's plans or forecast commercial success. No simulator or website has been built yet.

## What the initial audit changes

Can-Am's regional websites list Defender in both pilot countries. The decision must therefore concern further expansion, not first entry. Comparable annual sales of **utility side-by-sides** have not yet been verified for both countries. No market scores or rankings are justified yet.

The framework also needs a customer correction: a farm/business use case calls for enterprise purchasing capacity or an economic-usefulness proxy. Household GDP per capita alone is too weak to represent farm purchasing decisions.

## A manageable first working week

1. Review the scope and supply the assignment instructions (30–45 minutes).
2. Read the network-model abstract and obtain the full article through the university library; write a 150-word application note (60–90 minutes).
3. Review the OECD guide's normalization, weighting and sensitivity sections and identify two model risks (60–90 minutes).
4. Investigate the two countries' utility-vehicle category data using the audit's search record (two sessions of 60 minutes).
5. Record a retain/revise decision and connect the repository to GitHub (30–60 minutes).

Time allocations are planning suggestions, not course requirements. The research decision is whether the same useful demand measure can be collected in both countries. Stop widening the country list until that is resolved.

## Evidence conventions

- **Verified-source fact:** checked against identified source material by AI; student review is separately recorded.
- **Theory:** a proposition from an identified academic source, with access/read status.
- **Proposed analytical decision:** a scope, proxy, rubric or weight choice awaiting student adoption.
- **AI interpretation:** an inference or suggested application, never a substitute for factual evidence.

`research/observations.json` contains nulls for unknown numerical values. Null is not zero. Source records include limitations and publication-date uncertainty. Only source links and short research notes are included; copyrighted articles are not copied into the repository.
